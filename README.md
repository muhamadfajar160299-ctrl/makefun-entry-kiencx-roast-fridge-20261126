A one-line roast of a smart fridge, for the make.fun competition
[Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9).

## The entry

> My smart fridge held my leftovers hostage until I paid its ransom: a firmware update and a 47-page privacy policy.

One line, 114 characters. The fridge as ransomware: it locks the food
away until you pay up with a firmware update and a privacy policy.

## How it works

Run `main.py` (Python 3, standard library only). It writes the single
roast line, byte-exact and deterministic, to `entry.txt` (and to
`/output/entry.txt` when run by the grader).
